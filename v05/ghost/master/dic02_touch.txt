＃ v0.5.7 touch semantics are preserved in the Bridge.
＃ Only recognised touch-collision MOVE events are transported.
＃ MouseLeave / MouseLeaveAll are LOCAL visual housekeeping only.
＃ They never go to Bridge/LLM and never participate in gesture intensity.
＃ During talking/choosing/visible balloon, Leave returns no script at all.
＃ Local reflex timing:
＃ - Head/Hair keep Runtime's sustained-touch timing.
＃ - Bust hover is transient: first MOVE switches to the local surprised reflex and leaving restores normally.
＃ - Bust click is latched: single/double click keeps the surprised reflex after MouseLeave until the formal Runtime/LLM presentation owns the expression; system balloon close/timeout clears the latch.
＃ - Bust single-click semantics are deferred briefly so a double click becomes canonical grab without leaking a preceding light_touch.
＃ - Runtime v0.5.24 compatibility: Bust double-click is transported as legacy POINTER|double; Bridge v0.5.25 rewrites the outbound LLM semantic event from heavy_tap to grab.
＃ - Book single click reacts visually immediately; semantic meaning remains neutral look_at.
